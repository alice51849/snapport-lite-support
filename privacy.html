The Android version of Snapport Lite follows the same design: templates, face alignment, background tools, and export/print layout are the same, and your photos stay on your device and are never uploaded. Android has no App Intents, Widget, or iCloud equivalent, so those entry points do not exist on Android; every other privacy practice on this page applies equally.
