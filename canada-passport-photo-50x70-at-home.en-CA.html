Canadian Passport-Size Photo at Home: 50×70 mm on Your iPhone

If your Canadian application asks for a 50×70 mm photo, you can take it at home and size it on your iPhone. Here is what Snapport Lite can and cannot do, answered from its App Store listing.

  • What it does
  • Questions

What Snapport Lite does

  • 50×70 mm preset: one of 35 built-in presets, alongside 35×45 mm and 2×2 in, or set your own size in mm, inches or pixels.
  • Face guides: on-device face detection lines up head-top, eye-line and chin guides.
  • Background: switch to white, light grey, blue, red or a custom colour.
  • Print: several copies on one 4×6 in, A4 or Letter sheet with cut guides.

Questions

Can Snapport Lite make a 50×70 mm photo?

Yes. 50×70 mm is one of the built-in presets. If you need a different size, set a custom size in mm, inches or pixels, with your own DPI. Snapport Lite only edits your photo; it does not guarantee acceptance and is not affiliated with any government, so check the latest official requirements before you submit.

My wall at home isn't white. What can I do?

Switch the background to white or light grey (or blue, red or a custom colour), compare before and after, and refine the edges. Gentle brightness, contrast, warmth and sharpness controls keep you looking natural.

How do I get my head the right size in the 50×70 mm frame?

On-device face detection shows head-top, eye-line and chin guides. Pinch, drag and rotate to fine-tune the crop.

Can I print several copies on a 4×6 in sheet?

Yes. Lay out print sheets on 4×6 in, A4 or Letter paper, with several copies per page and cut guides. You can also export a single photo as JPEG, PNG or PDF and save it to Photos or Files, or share it.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Photos are processed on your device and never uploaded, and the app works offline. The optional template update check only downloads a public list of photo sizes and sends no personal data.

Who is Snapport Lite for?

It suits anyone in Canada who wants to take a 50×70 mm or 35×45 mm photo at home, fix the background and print copies on a 4×6 in sheet without uploading their face anywhere.
