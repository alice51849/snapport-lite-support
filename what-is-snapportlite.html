What is Snapport Lite?

Snapport Lite is an iPhone app that makes ID photos at home. It crops your portrait to the exact size you pick, from 35 built-in presets for 28 countries and regions (such as 2×2 in, 35×45 mm, 33×48 mm and 50×70 mm) or your own size in mm, inches or pixels. On-device face detection shows head-top, eye and chin guides, and you export JPEG, PNG or PDF or print several copies on one 4×6 in, A4 or Letter sheet. It only edits your photo; passports, visas and ID cards are issued by the authority you apply to, and acceptance is not guaranteed.

Who is Snapport Lite for?

People who need a passport, visa, ID, resume or application photo and would rather not go to a photo studio, people who need an unusual size, and anyone who wants the photo processed on the phone: photos are never uploaded and the app works offline.

How does Snapport Lite charge?

The download is free, and every size preset and editing tool is free to use. Your first export is included. A one-time purchase unlocks unlimited exports. The listing states there is no subscription, no ads, no tracking and no account. Prices differ by country, so check the App Store page for the current price.

How is Snapport Lite different from other ID photo apps?

Based on what the listing describes: it combines many country presets with fully custom sizes and DPI; face guides run on the device; it lays out print sheets with several copies and cut guides on 4×6 in, A4 or Letter paper; photos stay on the phone; and it is sold as a one-time unlock rather than a subscription.
