ID Photo App: Questions to Ask Before You Download

Need a 2×2 in photo for a passport or visa application, a resume headshot, or a size your form spells out in millimeters? Here are the questions people ask before downloading, answered from the Snapport Lite App Store listing.

  • What it does
  • Questions

What Snapport Lite does

  • Exact sizes: 35 built-in presets for 28 countries and regions, or your own size in mm, inches or pixels.
  • Face guides: on-device face detection lines up head-top, eye-line and chin guides.
  • Background: keep the original or switch to white, light gray, blue, red or a custom color.
  • Export and print: JPEG, PNG or PDF, or several copies on one 4×6 in, A4 or Letter sheet.

Questions

Can I make a 2×2 inch photo for a passport application?

Yes. 2×2 in is one of the built-in presets, alongside 35×45 mm, 33×48 mm and 50×70 mm. Snapport Lite only edits your photo; it does not guarantee acceptance and is not affiliated with any government, so check the official requirements before you submit.

What if the size I need isn't in the list?

Set a custom size in mm, inches or pixels, with your own DPI and background color.

How do I get my head the right size in the frame?

On-device face detection shows head-top, eye-line and chin guides. Pinch, drag and rotate to fine-tune the crop.

My wall at home isn't white. Can I still use the photo?

You can switch the background to white, light gray, blue, red or a custom color, compare before and after, and refine the edges. Gentle brightness, contrast, warmth and sharpness controls keep you looking natural.

Can I print several copies on one sheet?

Yes. Lay out print sheets on 4×6 in, A4 or Letter paper, with several copies per page and cut guides. You can also export a single photo as JPEG, PNG or PDF and save it to Photos or Files, or share it.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Photos are processed on your device and never uploaded. The optional template update check only downloads a public list of photo sizes and sends no personal data.

Good to know: unlimited exports are unlocked with a one-time purchase. No subscription, no ads, no tracking, no account.
